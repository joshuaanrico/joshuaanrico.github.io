About

I started in QA. For two years at Blitz Electric Mobility in Jakarta I wrote test plans, automated regression tests and ran UAT with the product team.

Now I build the products myself. I came to Melbourne for a Master of IT at Monash on an LPDP scholarship, and alongside it I've been building client platforms at Ailevate.

On team projects I usually end up running the work as well: splitting it into cards on a Kanban board, keeping the documentation, and making sure we ship on time.

I'm looking for a frontend, full-stack or QA role in Australia, starting from December 2026.

  1. Tested before it ships

    Vitest for the logic that matters, Playwright for the flows users actually click through. A habit from my QA years.

  2. Front to back

    On FlowIQ and Emberhawk I built the interface, the API and the deployment, so I can follow a bug through the whole stack.

  3. Leading the work, not just coding it

    On Emberhawk and Sit&Fit I planned the work on a Kanban board, split it into cards and kept the docs up to date. At work that's Jira and Confluence.

  4. Built for real conditions

    Bad signal, many roles, many devices. Emberhawk works offline in the hills; Lalita installs on a parent's phone.
